#  Felipe Fogaça

**`Software Engineer · Infrastructure & DevOps`**

Software and infrastructure engineer leading the development and operations of a university technology organization, responsible for the full lifecycle of its self-hosted platform, from architecture and deployment to monitoring and incident response. Designed and maintained 10+ production services under a single sign-on identity layer, with group-based access control, multi-factor authentication for administrators and automated user provisioning. Proven track record of improving reliability through automated backups, health monitoring and root-cause analysis of production incidents, and of reducing delivery time with CI/CD pipelines and end-to-end testing across desktop, tablet and mobile. Skilled in PHP, TypeScript and SQL, containerized architectures, reverse proxies, DNS and Linux server administration. Currently focused on building an internal knowledge platform with role-based content access and automated synchronization across systems.
